# The Book of Suhail: Gemini prompts for the nine painted pages

Version 2, 12 Nov 2026. Replaces round 6 in IMAGE_PROMPTS.md. Written for Google Gemini (paid plan).
Each prompt below is complete: copy one whole block, paste it, generate. One image per prompt.

## How to use

1. Generate **page 1 first**. Make a few versions and pick the one you like most. It sets the style and the two characters for the whole book.
2. For pages 2 to 9, **attach your chosen page 1** to the chat and paste the prompt. Each prompt already says "same painting style and the same two characters as the attached image".
3. Check each result against the short list under its prompt before you accept it.
4. Save the winners as `lore_prologue_v01.jpg`, `lore_age1_v01.jpg` ... `lore_age7_v01.jpg`, `lore_yours_v01.jpg` and send them to me. Dropped into `assets/lore/`, they replace the code drawings automatically.

Size: landscape 4:3, as large as Gemini gives (2048 px wide or more is ideal).

The two characters who appear on every page, always seen from behind or from far away:

- **The traveller** is the player. A plain sand-coloured hooded cloak, no face ever shown, so any player can be them.
- **Rawi** is our storyteller (fictional). Elderly, white beard, white thobe, brown cloak (bisht), white headcloth with a black cord.

## Page 1. The Star That Remembers  (`lore_prologue`)

```
Create a painted storybook illustration, landscape 4:3.

Scene: the Arabian desert on a late-summer night, an hour before dawn. Two small figures stand on the crest of a dune, seen from behind, looking at one brilliant white-gold star that has just risen low over the southern horizon. The star is far brighter than every other star and throws a soft path of light across the dunes toward the viewer, like a road. The first figure is a traveller in a plain sand-coloured hooded cloak, face never visible. The second is an elderly storyteller with a white beard, white robe, brown cloak and a white headcloth held by a black cord, one hand raised toward the star. A resting camel beside them. Thousands of faint stars, deep indigo sky fading to amber at the horizon, long soft shadows on rippled sand.

Mood: wonder, stillness, the start of a journey.

Style: painterly historical illustration, oil and gouache on textured canvas, visible confident brushstrokes, luminous and slightly magical but realistic, restrained palette of deep indigo, sand ochre, amber and warm white, hand-painted, museum quality. Keep the star and the figures in the middle band of the picture; nothing important in the top or bottom fifth.

Do not include: any text, letters, Arabic script, watermark, signature, border, frame, cartoon or anime style, 3D render, genie, magic lamp, flying carpet, palace, modern objects.
```

Check: one dominant star, low in the sky. Both figures from behind. No text anywhere.

## Page 6. The Song Above the Deep  (`lore_age5`)


## Page 8. Seven Hands, One Flag  (`lore_age7`)

```
Create a painted storybook illustration, landscape 4:3, in the same painting style and with the same two characters as the attached image.

Scene: a bright winter morning on the Gulf coast, 2 December 1971. A tall white flagpole stands on open sand, and the flag of the United Arab Emirates opens in the wind at the top: a vertical red band at the pole side, and three equal horizontal bands of green on top, white in the middle and black at the bottom. A crowd of men, women and children in traditional Gulf dress stands around the pole, every one of them seen from behind, looking up. At the very front of the picture, also from behind, are the traveller in the sand-coloured hooded cloak and the elderly storyteller in white robe and brown cloak. Behind the flag: a low coastal town of coral-stone houses with square wind towers, date palms, and wooden dhows at anchor in a calm blue bay. In the pale morning sky, the same single brilliant star is still faintly visible.

Mood: pride, quiet joy, a held breath as the flag opens.

Style: painterly historical illustration, oil and gouache on textured canvas, visible confident brushstrokes, luminous, restrained palette of clear morning blue, sand ochre, coral white, and the four flag colours as the strongest colours in the picture. Keep the flag, the crowd and the star in the middle band of the picture.

Do not include: any recognisable real person, any face turned to the viewer, any ruler or official on a stage, any text, letters, banners with writing, watermark, signature, border, skyscrapers, cars, cartoon or anime style, 3D render.
```

Check carefully: the flag must be exactly right (red vertical band at the pole; green, white, black from top to bottom). Regenerate if it is not. Nobody faces the viewer and no ruler is shown: members of reigning families never appear in the game.
