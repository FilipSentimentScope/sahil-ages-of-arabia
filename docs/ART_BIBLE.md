# Art Bible

Status: **outline**. Written by the Creative agent in Phase 3, approved by Filip at Gate 1.
No volume asset generation before approval.

## Fixed inputs (already decided)

- Aesthetic: an interactive historical painting. Painterly 2D, visible brushwork, rich textures,
  atmospheric light, rich but balanced colour, hand-painted look across all assets.
- Avoid: generic mobile-game look, cartoon styling, inconsistent AI output, generic
  "Arabian Nights" fantasy, one homogeneous "MENA" look.
- Portrait orientation, one-handed play; layout width up to 480 px; buildings readable at about 56 px.
- Setting for the slice: lower Gulf coast, Age V (about 1650 to 1929).
- Key art gets human paint-over (quality and copyright).

## Sections to write

1. Colour palette: base, accent, light and shadow, per-age shift (Age V to VI preview).
2. Illustration style: brushwork, edges, texture, level of detail by asset size.
3. Character design: proportions, faces, dress by period and place, how real people are shown
   (respectful, based on sources; no invented likeness claims).
4. Environment design: architecture of the period (barasti, coral stone, wind towers), shoreline,
   boats (types to verify with the Historian), terrain tiles.
5. Interface visual language: frames, ornament sources (sadu and talli patterns, geometry),
   type pairing for Latin and Arabic, icon style, readability rules.
6. Animation style: subtle, painterly motion (water, palms, smoke, cloth); fight tableaux.
7. Texture and lighting: time of day, light direction, paper or canvas grain.
8. Reference pack: each reference with source and period, checked by the Historian.
9. Banned elements: list of clichés and anachronisms (to compile with the Historian).
10. Prompt templates: Midjourney and Scenario base prompts, negative prompts, style references,
    and the trained-model plan.
