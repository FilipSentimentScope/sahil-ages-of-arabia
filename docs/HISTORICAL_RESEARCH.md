# Historical research and accuracy

## Rules

1. Never fabricate facts, dates, quotations, names or sources.
2. Every Archive page and history question cites at least one credible source
   (academic book or article, museum or heritage authority, primary source). Record author,
   title, publisher or site, year, page or URL.
3. Where interpretations differ, say so in the text ("historians debate...").
4. AI agents draft; only a human historian can set a record to `historian-reviewed`.
5. No historical text appears in a public playtest before human review.
6. Real-people policy, GDD section 10: groups A, B, C. Group C figures never appear as characters.
   No invented dialogue for any real person. Matters of faith stay in the Archive in factual language.
7. Rival towns are invented types, never real emirates, cities or tribes.

## Content record statuses

`draft` (agent-written, sourced) → `designer-approved` (fits the game) →
`historian-reviewed` (human historian) → `approved` (Filip).
